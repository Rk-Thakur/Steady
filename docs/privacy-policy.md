# Steady: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Steady is a budgeting app that shows you one number: how much is safe to spend today. It was built so your money stays private: **everything you enter stays on your phone.**

This policy explains what that means in practice. If anything here is unclear, write to **[SUPPORT EMAIL]**.

---

## The short version

- **No account.** You never sign up, sign in, or give us your name or email to use Steady.
- **No servers.** Steady has no server of its own. Your budget, entries, bills, goals and split expenses are never sent to us or anyone else.
- **No tracking.** No analytics, no advertising, no tracking across apps or websites.
- **Encrypted on your phone.** Steady's database is encrypted, and its key is kept in your phone's secure storage (the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore).
- **You're in control.** You choose if and when to make a backup, export a file, or share something, and where it goes.

---

## What Steady stores, and where

Everything below is stored **only on your device**, inside Steady's encrypted database:

- Money amounts you enter: your balance, income, spending, and the daily number history
- Bills and subscriptions, with their amounts and due dates
- Spending categories and optional monthly limits
- Goals and how much is saved toward them
- Paycheck Vault settings
- Split expenses: names of the people you split with (just the names you type), groups, shared expenses and settlements
- Optional details you add to a spend: where you spent, a mood, and whether it was planned
- Your settings: currency, payday, reminders, theme, and similar

We (the developer) cannot see any of it. There is no copy anywhere except on your phone, and in backup files you choose to create.

### App lock, Face ID and fingerprint

If you turn on App lock, your PIN is stored in your phone's secure storage, not in the database and never off the device.

If you also turn on Face ID or fingerprint unlock, your phone checks your face or fingerprint itself. **Steady never receives, stores or sees your face or fingerprint data**; the operating system only tells Steady "unlocked" or "not unlocked".

### Reminders

Reminders (for example "Log your spends", "Bill due soon" and "Payday") are **scheduled on your phone** using your phone's notification system. They are not sent from a server. You can turn each one off in Steady under Settings › Reminders, or turn off all notifications for Steady in your phone's Settings.

## Things that leave your phone only when you choose

### Backups

You can create a backup file (`.steady`). It is **encrypted with a password you choose** (AES-256-GCM, with the key derived from your password using Argon2id). You decide where the file goes, for example your Files app, a cloud drive, or email. Without the password the file can't be read. **We can't recover a lost password**, because we never have it.

### Exports

You can export your entries as a CSV file or a summary as a PDF. **These files are not encrypted.** They contain the details shown in them, so keep them somewhere private. Where they go is up to you.

### Nudges to people you split with

On Split expenses, "Send a nudge" opens your phone's share sheet with a short message (for example who owes what). You pick the app and the person; nothing is sent until you send it in that app.

### Feedback email

"Help & feedback" opens your own email app with our address filled in. We only receive what you choose to send. We use it only to reply to you and improve Steady, and we don't share it.

## What we don't do

- We don't collect personal information, usage data, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers.
- We don't use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.
- We don't sell, rent or share data, because we don't have any.
- We don't use cookies or similar technologies.

---

## Keeping or deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you remove it:

- **Delete everything inside Steady:** Settings › Backup & export › Delete all my data. This erases the database and your PIN on this phone.
- **Uninstall Steady:** removes the app and its data from your phone.
- **Backup or export files** you created are separate files: delete them wherever you saved them.

Because we don't hold a copy, we can't restore data for you. Make a backup if you want to be able to recover it.

## Children

Steady is a general-audience budgeting app and isn't directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.
